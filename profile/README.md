# SEE-Insight

## About Us

SEE-Insight is an undergraduate research group at Michigan State University that provides students with opportunities to gain hands-on experience working on research projects.

Our work involves applying computing, data analysis, and software development to research problems across different disciplines. Students collaborate with researchers, develop technical skills, and contribute to projects that support ongoing research.

## Our Projects

SEE-Insight students contribute to a variety of projects, including:

- Scientific image analysis and automated data processing
- Research software development and computational tools
- Machine learning and data-driven research
- Software tools that support research workflows

Projects vary in scope and focus, giving students opportunities to explore different areas of research while gaining practical experience.

## Collaboration and Learning

SEE-Insight emphasizes learning through hands-on research and collaboration. Students participate in regular meetings, share project updates, learn from other researchers, and document their progress.

The group encourages good software development practices, including version control, documentation, testing, and reproducibility.
